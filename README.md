# 🧠 BUILDMIND-360

### AI-Powered Customer Feedback → Product Intelligence Platform

> **Turn thousands of customer reviews into clear product decisions.**

BUILDMIND-360 is an AI-powered product intelligence platform designed to automatically understand large-scale customer feedback, discover recurring problems, identify patterns, reason about possible root causes, prioritize issues, and recommend what product teams should build next.

Instead of forcing Product Managers to manually read thousands of reviews, BUILDMIND-360 transforms raw customer feedback into **evidence-backed, actionable product insights**.

---

## 🚀 What Problem Are We Solving?

Modern applications receive huge amounts of customer feedback from:

- Google Play Store reviews
- Instagram
- WhatsApp
- Support tickets
- Surveys
- Emails
- Chat logs
- Social media
- Call-center feedback

Reading and analyzing all of this manually is:

- ⏳ Time-consuming
- 📊 Difficult to scale
- 🧩 Hard to identify recurring patterns
- ❌ Easy to miss important problems
- 🤔 Difficult to decide what should be built next

### BUILDMIND-360 solves this by creating an automated feedback intelligence pipeline.

```text
Customer Feedback
        ↓
Data Collection
        ↓
Database Storage
        ↓
Data Preprocessing
        ↓
Semantic Retrieval
        ↓
AI / LLM Analysis

Problem Discovery
        ↓
Root Cause Reasoning
        ↓
Severity & Impact Analysis
        ↓
Priority Ranking
        ↓
Solution Generation
        ↓
Product Recommendations
        ↓
Product Manager
